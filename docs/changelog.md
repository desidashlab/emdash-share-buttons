## 0.1.0

- First release. Share buttons for X, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Telegram, Bluesky, Threads, Reddit and email, plus copy link and the device share sheet.
- Position at the end of the post, under the title, both, or floating at the left edge on wide screens.
- Brand colours or an outline in the theme's text colour, with or without names.
- Optional utm tracking tags on shared links and a "via" username for X.
- Every page with a post area, or only addresses starting with a path. Setup tab shows whether the site is loading the buttons.
