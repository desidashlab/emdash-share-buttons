## Two steps

1. In your EmDash admin open **Registry**, search for **Share Buttons**, and click **Install**. It asks for no permissions.
2. Open **Share Buttons** in the left menu, go to the **Setup** tab, and paste the short block it shows into the head of your base layout, usually `src/layouts/Base.astro`. If someone else built your site, send them that block.

The buttons appear at the end of every post within a minute. On the **Buttons** tab you can pick the networks, move the buttons, switch to outline colours or add names next to the icons.
